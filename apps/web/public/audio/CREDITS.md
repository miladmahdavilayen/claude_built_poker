# Music credits

Both tracks are by **Alex McCulloch** (Pro Sensory on OpenGameArt), dedicated
to the public domain under CC0 1.0 — free for any use; the composer asks to be
credited, and is, here and in the app's sound note.

- `login-piano.mp3` — "jazz improvisation looped",
  https://opengameart.org/content/jazz-improvisation-looped
- `lobby-swing.mp3` — "October Jazz" (oct_2_fixed),
  https://opengameart.org/content/october-jazz

Both were made quieter (loudness-normalized to about −23 LUFS), their top end
softened with a gentle low-pass, and re-encoded at 96 kbps.
