# Test players' camera clips

`cam-1` … `cam-7` (each as WebM — VP9/Opus — and MP4 — H.264/AAC, for the browsers
without WebM) are the owner's test players' "cameras" (see
`src/testBotCameras.ts`): six-second clips, cropped to a camera's upright 3:4,
240×320, their sound kept but quiet (about −28 dB mean, mono).
